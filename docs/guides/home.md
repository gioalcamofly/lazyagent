# Home

The desktop app opens on **Home**: one line per work item, what needs you first.

Its guide is now **[How lazyagent works](how-lazyagent-works.md)**: the zones, the ball, every state, verb and action with its key, your rules and the facts, with screenshots. That page is generated from the rules catalog by `lazyagent rules doc --write`, so it never drifts from what the engine does; the same page is in the app (Help → How lazyagent works, `F1`; `?` on Home opens it at Home).

In short, since the second UX review: `a` approves and `⇧D` denies an agent's command (a deny waits 5 s for its Undo); `y` explains and `n` opens the notes on every line. The key bar names only the selected line's keys; `?` lists them all. A deep item under a quicker one (a changes requested under a red CI) also shows in To sit down with, and stale worktrees fold at the end of In flight with their uncommitted files. Since I4g that deep item is a line of its own there (`↳`): `j`/`k`, Tab and `3` reach it, Enter expands it on that item (a brief: pick with 1-4, answer with Ctrl+Enter, `v` for the decision view), and in any expanded line a brief behind the top one has **Answer here**. A red CI made only of checks on `[inbox] auto_delegate_skip_checks` is not auto-delegated; its line says so.

A teammate's PR that asks you (or a team of yours) for a review is a Round line of its own, with no worktree needed: **Review** · "review requested by <author> · <title>". `p` opens the PR; expanded, it says when you were asked and through which team. It goes once you review (approve, request changes or comment), the request is withdrawn or the PR closes; dismissed or parked, it reads "review requested · set aside" in In flight. It is never delegated. A teammate's branch you checked out reads as theirs ("ana's PR"), not as yours to merge or to find a reviewer for.

Since I4i, `d` (Delegate) hands every delegable item of a line to its agent in one letter: the button reads **Delegate all (2)** when there are several, and in the expanded line each of them has "delegate this one" to send it alone. A letter waits for the agent's turn to end; while it waits, the line says so ("waiting for a5 to be free (possibly hanged)"), and after a minute the expanded line offers **Send anyway**, or open its terminal (`o`). Pressing Delegate again adds to the waiting letter (or sends a follow-up): nothing is dropped, and the timeline counts repeats ("×3"). The bar under an expanded line keeps the primary verbs (at most six); **Why?** (`w`), **Notes** (`n`) and the rest are under **More…**; the keys work as before and the key bar and `?` still name them. A Claude agent restored with `--resume` reads idle at its prompt, not "possibly hanged".

**What to review.** On your own PR's Review line ("new commits since your approval", or the commits since it was marked ready), the expanded line lists what you have to look at: the commits since your approval, else since the PR was last marked ready, else (a PR opened ready) "since the PR was opened", newest first with their short sha, subject, author and date, at most 15 and "and N more". A merge of the base that changes nothing of yours (a clean "Update branch", or one where only generated files differ) is listed as "base merge, not counted". **Compare on GitHub**, in the line itself (no new button in the bar), opens `<repo>/compare/<from>...<head>` from that same point (the base branch for a PR opened ready). Approve (`⇧A`) records the head shown; when the PR moved since, it is refused and the line refreshes to the new head. Delegate on a line whose agent is gone restores it (or starts one) and delegates in one step: the line reads the delegation from the start, and the letter waits until the agent is at its prompt.

**The ball principle.** What the branch's agent can fix (a conflict, CI red, Sync with base) does not interrupt you while that agent has the ball: it runs, or its `status_report` holds it (ball `agent`, or `others` with an until). The line stays In flight as "agent a5 · conflict · handling the conflict · until 14:05", and is back in your Round when the until lapses, the agent dies, or it asks you something. Approvals, questions, briefs, your own Review and Merge, and review requests still interrupt. A Hanged? waits while the agent's report holds, and an Exited goes once another agent runs on that worktree.

**GitHub unavailable.** When `gh` cannot reach GitHub (no network, logged out), a yellow line under the top bar says "GitHub unavailable since HH:MM · PR data from HH:MM" (`gh`'s error on hover). The lines keep their last PR, CI and review facts meanwhile: a review waiting for you stays in the Round, and a branch whose PR was never fetched does not read as "review: agent finished" (`pr.exists` is unknown, not false). The engine retries 1, 2, 4, 8, then every 10 minutes; the line goes as soon as GitHub answers.
